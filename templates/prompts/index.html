About the project

What is PromptHub?

PromptHub is a community-driven platform built with Django. It lets anyone create an account, publish AI prompts, organize them by category, rate others' work, and leave comments. Think of it as GitHub — but for prompts.

Store Prompts

Write, save and organize your best AI prompts. Add descriptions and categories.

Discover

Search and browse prompts from the community. Filter by category, author or rating.

Rate & Review

Rate prompts from 1–5 stars and leave comments. Help the best content rise to the top.

Author Profiles

Every author has a public profile showing their prompts and activity.

Categories

Prompts organized by categories — coding, writing, marketing, and more.

Secure Accounts

Full auth system — register, login, update your profile or delete your account.
